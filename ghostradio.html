    About this project

    Each relic is a station. Stay on its frequency and its story plays in first person. As time passes, the signal weakens, until the last message and then silence. You don't read that a machine went quiet. You hear it happen.

    Mars band: Sojourner 90.0, Phoenix 93.8, Spirit 97.6, Opportunity 101.4, InSight 105.2. Moon band: ALSEP 93.0, Apollo 17 Rover 101.0. Use the Band button to switch.

    Facts & sources

    Dates are based on NASA/JPL material. The spoken lines are creative first-person retellings, not real recordings. "My battery is low and it's getting dark" is a widely shared poetic paraphrase of Opportunity's final data, not a literal message.
